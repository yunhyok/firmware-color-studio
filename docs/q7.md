# PENTAX Q7 camera pack

The `pentax-q7` pack makes DCP (and DCP + XMP) profiles for the PENTAX Q7 from the colour tables and
calculations of its firmware 1.01. It is selected in the camera menu at the top of the window, or with
`--camera pentax-q7` on the command line. The working record of the reverse engineering, with how each
finding was obtained, is [research/q7-port.md](research/q7-port.md).

## What the pack reproduces

| Stage | Firmware routine | In Studio |
|---|---|---|
| Colour matrix: saturation (or monochrome filter) × Custom Image × sensor matrix, 1.0 = 256 | `ColorMatchingService_MakeColorMatrix` | `Q7Engine.color_matrix` |
| YC conversion with hue rotation, AdobeRGB variant, toning offsets | same routine | `Q7Engine.yc`, `chroma_offset` |
| Gamma table from key and contrast (4096 entries) | `GammaCorrectionService_CalcGammaCorrectionTable` | `Q7Engine.gamma` |
| Reversal Film gamma table | `GammaCorrectionService_CalcGammaCorrectionTableReversal` | `Q7Engine.gamma` |
| Dark chroma suppression | `ColorMatchingService_MakeColorReproductionLutParam` | `CameraColour.shadow_chroma` |
| Factory settings of each Custom Image | `cntCustomImageUnit<Mode>` records | `Q7Engine.factory_settings` |
| DNG colour matrices (illuminant A, D65) | `arcnlDNGColorMatrix1`, `2` | `Q7Engine.dng_color_matrices` |

Ten Custom Images are offered: Natural, Bright, Portrait, Landscape, Vibrant, Radiant, Muted, Bleach
Bypass, Reversal Film and Monochrome. Saturation, hue, key and contrast follow the firmware's rules:
Muted and Bleach Bypass have a toning colour in place of hue, Monochrome has key and contrast, and
Reversal Film has no settings. The Q7 has no highlight and shadow contrast settings. Cross Processing
uses per-channel curves and is not included; neither are the monochrome filter effect and monochrome
toning settings (the factory values are used).

## How it was checked

- **The integer calculations equal the firmware's.** `tools/q7/check_port.py` runs the three routines of
  the unpacked firmware in a CPU emulator and compares every output value with `q7_engine.py`:
  276 colour settings (colour matrices, YC matrix, chroma offsets) and 906 gamma tables of 4096 entries
  are identical. This needs the firmware file and is not part of the automatic tests; `tests/test_q7.py`
  keeps values from that comparison.
- **The tables were extracted twice.** The unpacker in `tools/q7/` and the one in
  [pentax-q7-firmware-analysis](https://github.com/yunhyok/pentax-q7-firmware-analysis) were written
  independently; the unpacked segments have the same SHA-256 and the exported tables are byte-identical.
- **The D65 DNG matrix equals a published one.** The firmware's `arcnlDNGColorMatrix2` is the matrix
  LibRaw lists for the Q-S1, which has the Q7's sensor.
- **The same engine family.** Factory settings and toning offsets read from the Q7 firmware equal those of
  the K-3 Mark III pack for the Custom Images both cameras have.

## What is not verified

- **No Q7 photograph has been used.** Nothing has been compared with a Q7 camera JPEG or with Lightroom
  rendering a Q7 DNG. The profiles follow the firmware's parameters; whether Lightroom then matches the
  camera has not been measured.
- **Exposure anchor.** `BaselineExposureOffset` is 0 for this pack. The K-3 Mark III pack uses +0.5 EV
  from a comparison of camera files; the corresponding Q7 value needs a Q7 DNG and the camera JPEG of the
  same shot. Until then Q7 profiles can be uniformly brighter or darker than the camera's rendering.
- **The camera interpolates a table.** The Q7 does not apply matrix and gamma as separate stages: it
  samples "gamma(dark chroma suppression(matrix × RGB))" at 9 × 9 × 9 points into a three-dimensional
  table and interpolates between them in the hardware. Studio uses the sampled function itself. Colours
  at the table points are the same; in between, the camera can differ slightly. How the hardware
  interpolates is not known.
- **AdobeRGB.** The firmware changes only the YC matrix and the toning offsets for AdobeRGB; no routine
  that re-encodes the gamma table for AdobeRGB was found. The pack does the same.
- **RAW input.** Studio reads K-3 Mark III RAW files only. With the Q7 selected, the preview passes the
  K-3 Mark III image through both sensor matrices; it shows the look, not a Q7 exposure. Importing a body
  calibration from a Q7 DNG is not supported yet.
